Mafia Online

    An online Mafia party game where players receive secret roles, work together to identify 
    the Mafia member(s), and try to win.

How to Play

    Join a game with other players.

    Receive a secret role. (Mafia, Villagers, or Doctor)

    Mafia: eliminates players
  
    Doctor: can choose 1 player to save each round (can be yourself)
  
    Villagers: Discuss and figure out who the Mafia is

    Work with other players to figure out who the Mafia member(s) are.

    Use discussion and voting to eliminate suspects.

    Try to achieve your team's win condition!

Features

    Secret player roles

    Multiplayer gameplay

    Discussion and voting

    Compete as the Mafia or the innocent players

About

    This project is an online version of the classic Mafia party game.
